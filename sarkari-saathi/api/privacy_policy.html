Sarkari Saathi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sarkari Saathi ("the app") helps Indian citizens find government welfare schemes they may be eligible for. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Email address — used only to log you in via a one-time code (no password is ever stored).
  • Eligibility profile (optional, only if you save a profile or enable notifications) — age, gender, state, social category, income bracket, BPL status, and disability status, all entered by you. This is used solely to match you against government scheme criteria.
  • Push notification token (only if you enable notifications) — a device identifier used to deliver alerts about newly added schemes matching your saved profile.
  • Notification history — a record of which alerts were sent to you, so you can view past notifications in the app.

Why we collect it

Solely to operate the app's core features: matching you to relevant government schemes, letting you save profiles for yourself or family members, and notifying you when a new matching scheme is added. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

Who your data passes through

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. Each only receives the minimum data needed to perform its function:

  • NVIDIA — processes your questions to the AI assistant to generate answers. Questions are not stored by us beyond the request.
  • Brevo — sends the one-time login code to your email address.
  • Google Firebase / Firestore — stores your push notification token, synced eligibility profile (if notifications are enabled), and notification history.

Data retention and deletion

Your synced eligibility profile, push token, and notification history are deleted from our servers immediately when you log out of the app. Locally saved profiles on your device are deleted when you remove them in the Saved tab, or when you uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • You can use the app's scheme search and AI assistant without logging in or saving any profile.
  • You can log out at any time to remove your synced data from our servers.
  • You can disable notifications, or simply not save an eligibility profile, to avoid any data being synced.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to server-stored data is gated by a session token issued only after you verify your email via a one-time code.

Children's privacy

Sarkari Saathi is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.
